About DashStats

DashStats collects public DashCraft track and leaderboard information. Choose verified tracks for a quicker scan; all public tracks can take longer to load.

Quick manual: find a share link

Account lookup

  1. Open your DashCraft account page.
  2. Click the share icon in the top left.
  3. Copy the link and paste it into DashStats Account lookup. DashStats reads your user ID from the link.

Individual track

  1. Start playing the track you want to load.
  2. Click the … button while the track is playing.
  3. Press Share, copy the link, and paste it into DashStats Individual track.
